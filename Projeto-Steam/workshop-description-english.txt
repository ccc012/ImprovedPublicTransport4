[h1]Improved Public Transport 4[/h1]
[b]One mod instead of fifteen.[/b] Full control of transit lines: fleet size, vehicle types, stops, fares. Small mods unified.
[b]Version 4.8.5[/b] · Built for game version 1.21.1-f9

[h1]Why this exists[/h1]
Transit maintenance spiralled to [b]-42,000,000,000[/b] — two mods each resizing fleets independently, overwriting each other. This mod fixes the conflict by centralizing control.
[hr][/hr]
[h1]What you can do with it[/h1]
[h2]Take charge of any line[/h2]
Every line type gets: [b]fleet control[/b] (manual or budget-based), [b]vehicle selection[/b], [b]individual edits[/b], and [b]depot choice[/b].
[h2]Fix bunching[/h2]
Three anti-bunching levels: [b]Disabled[/b] (vanilla), [b]Prudent[/b] (spacing), [b]Realistic[/b] (true intervals). [b]Express[/b] mode skips empty stops.
[h2]Set your own fares[/h2]
Price each transport type in [b]Economy[/b] panel, with [b]day/night pricing[/b] options. Prices drive demand naturally.
[h2]See what is happening[/h2]
[b]Stop info[/b] (passengers, vehicle, crowding), [b]Train Display[/b] (live configurable overlay), [b]auto line colours/names[/b] for clarity, [b]Flight Tracker[/b] (arrivals/departures).
[h2]Clean up your network[/h2]
[b]Bulk line deletion[/b] (by type, confirmed), [b]elevated stop placement[/b], and [b]unstucker[/b] for stuck vehicles.
[h2]New in 4.8[/h2]
[b]Single Train Track AI[/b] prevents collisions on shared single-track segments. [b]Bus stop berth stacking[/b] gives buses separate berths. [b]Intercity Bus Control[/b] DLC check fixed. [b]Sub-Buildings Tabs[/b] stale-tab bug fixed. Fresh installs load [b]Safe[/b] profile; choose Recommended or Realistic, or customize. Plus performance improvements.

[h1]Requirements & compatibility[/h1]
[b]Harmony[/b] (ID 2040656402) required. [b]Not compatible with:[/b] IPT2, IPT3, Transport Lines Manager, standalone AutoLineBudget, or a standalone copy of any mod above — warns you in-game if found.
[hr][/hr]
[h1]Languages[/h1]
[b]All 23 languages fully translated[/b] — every string, not just the menus. Follows your game language, or pick one in the options.
[hr][/hr]
[h1]Getting started[/h1]
Subscribe [b]Harmony[/b], unsubscribe standalones, enable IPT4. Options: [b]Settings → Mod Settings → Improved Public Transport 4[/b]. Fares: [b]Economy[/b] panel. [b]Existing saves compatible[/b].
[hr][/hr]
[h1]Stability[/h1]
Stable. Runaway-maintenance bug fixed; damaged saves auto-repair. [b]Backup saves anyway[/b]. On issues, report [i]what you were doing[/i] and attach:
[code]%LOCALAPPDATA%\Colossal Order\Cities_Skylines\Cities_Data\output_log.txt[/code]
“Doesn’t work” alone cannot be diagnosed.
