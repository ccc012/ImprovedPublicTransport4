[h1]Update 4.8.9 is live[/h1]
Subscribers should get the update automatically (Steam may need a moment — restart the game if the version in Options still shows 4.8.8).

[b]What's new (short):[/b]
[b]Six feature master switches[/b] in Options (Unbunching, Budget, Auto Line Color, Train Display, Vehicle Editor, Stops & Stations) — off by default; turning one off locks its children and stops related patches.
[b]Thirteen rebindable hotkeys[/b] (line panel, unbunching, copy/paste, vehicle editor, Flight Tracker, prev/next vehicle, and more).
[b]Passenger destination display[/b] rebuilt as a clean-room map overlay (circles + counts). Still Experimental and off by default.
[b]Intercity Bus Control[/b] restores original station prefabs when disabled.
[b]Train Display[/b]: fixed interval per performance profile, scope dropdown (Selected / First person / Both).
[b]Compat tab[/b] lists recommended companion mods with Workshop links.
Thread-safety / save-data hardening; Express Bus pathfinding fails open.
