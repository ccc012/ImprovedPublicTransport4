[h1]Improved Public Transport 4[/h1]

[b]One mod instead of fifteen.[/b] IPT4 gives you real control over every transit line in your city — how many vehicles run, which ones, where they stop, what a ticket costs — and it folds in a whole shelf of small public-transport mods so they stop fighting each other.

[b]Version 4.3.7 · BETA[/b] · Built for game version 1.21.1-f9

[hr][/hr]

[h1]Why this exists[/h1]

This started as a bug hunt. A city was bleeding money — public transport maintenance had run away to roughly [b]-42,000,000,000[/b]. The cause was not one broken mod. It was two working mods both deciding, independently, how big each line's fleet should be. Each one kept overwriting the other, and the fleet — and its upkeep — grew without limit.

That is the kind of problem you cannot fix by disabling a mod, because both mods were doing their job correctly. So the fix was to put them in the same place, talking to one system instead of to the same hidden setting behind each other's back.

That is IPT4: the line-management features you already use, plus a set of small transit mods absorbed into one assembly where they coordinate properly.

[hr][/hr]

[h1]What you can do with it[/h1]

[h2]Take charge of any line[/h2]
Open any bus, tram, metro, train, ship, plane, monorail, cable car, taxi or tour line and you get a full control panel:

[list]
[*][b]Set the fleet size yourself[/b] — pick exactly how many vehicles run on a line, or let the mod size it from the budget. Your manual choice is remembered and never silently overridden.
[*][b]Choose which vehicles run[/b] — restrict a line to specific vehicle types, so your city trams stay on city routes and your intercity trains stay intercity.
[*][b]Edit vehicles individually[/b] — rename a vehicle, send it to the depot, or jump the camera to it.
[*][b]Depot control[/b] — decide which depot serves which line instead of letting the game guess.
[/list]

[h2]Fix bunching[/h2]
Every player knows the sight: four buses nose to tail, then nothing for ten minutes. IPT4 gives you unbunching with three levels you can actually reason about:

[list]
[*][b]Disabled[/b] — vanilla behaviour.
[*][b]Prudent[/b] — gentle spacing corrections. Safe on any city.
[*][b]Realistic[/b] — holds vehicles at stops to keep true intervals, the way a real operator would.
[/list]

Available separately for buses and for trams, plus [b]Express Bus Services[/b] and [b]Express Trams Services[/b] if you would rather have vehicles skip empty stops than wait at them.

[h2]Set your own fares[/h2]
The [b]Ticket Prices[/b] tab in the Economy panel lets you price each transport type independently, and the panel shows the resulting fare so you can see what you are actually charging. Optional [b]day and night pricing[/b], and price changes feed into passenger demand — cheap transit fills up, expensive transit empties out.

[h2]See what is happening[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Stop info panel[/b] — waiting passengers, next vehicle, and how crowded the stop is.
[*][b]Train Display[/b] — follow any vehicle and get a live route overlay showing the stop sequence and where you are on the line. Configurable position, size, transparency, which fields appear, update rate and colour theme.
[*][b]Flight Tracker[/b] — arrivals and departures for your airports.
[*][b]Automatic line colours and names[/b] — new lines get sensible names and distinct colours, so your transport map stays readable without manual work.
[/list]

[h2]Clean up your network[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Bulk line deletion[/b] — remove every line of a chosen type at once, with a confirmation step. Useful when you are redesigning a district.
[*][b]Better stop placement[/b] — stops snap to sensible positions instead of the middle of a junction, and you can place stops on elevated track.
[*][b]Unstucker[/b] — vehicles that get permanently stuck are released instead of blocking a route forever.
[/list]

[h1]Requirements[/h1]

[list]
[*][b]Harmony[/b] (ID 2040656402) — [b]required[/b]. Subscribe to it.
[*]Nothing else. Everything the mod needs is included in the download.
[/list]

[b]Not compatible with:[/b]
[list]
[*]Improved Public Transport 2 or 3 — IPT4 replaces them.
[*]Transport Lines Manager — both manage the same line data.
[*]Standalone AutoLineBudget.
[*]Any standalone copy of the mods in the list above.
[/list]

The mod will warn you in-game if it detects one of these.

[h1]Languages[/h1]

[b]All 23 languages are fully translated[/b] — every string in the mod, not just the menus.

English, Portuguese (Brazil and Portugal), Spanish, French, German, Italian, Dutch, Polish, Czech, Slovak, Turkish, Russian, Arabic, Urdu, Hindi, Bengali, Indonesian, Thai, Japanese, Korean, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional).

If something reads awkwardly in your language, that is worth reporting — the files are plain text on GitHub and a correction takes a minute.

The mod follows your game language by default, or you can pick one in the options panel. Translation corrections are welcome — see the GitHub link below.

[h1]Getting started[/h1]

[olist]
[*]Subscribe to [b]Harmony[/b] if you have not already.
[*]Unsubscribe from IPT2/IPT3 and from the standalone mods listed above.
[*]Subscribe to IPT4 and enable it in Content Manager.
[*]Load a city. Click any transit line to open the line panel.
[*]Options are under [b]Settings → Mod Settings → Improved Public Transport 4[/b]. Fares are in the game's [b]Economy[/b] panel.
[/olist]

Existing saves work. IPT4 reads the same save data blocks IPT2 and IPT3 wrote, so your per-line fleet sizes, depot assignments and vehicle restrictions carry over when you switch.

[h1]This release is BETA[/h1]

The mod is stable in normal play, but it changed a great deal recently and not every combination has been tested on every city. [b]Keep a backup save[/b] before you first load a large city with it.

If something goes wrong, the most useful thing you can do is say [i]what you were doing when it happened[/i] and attach your log:
[code]%LOCALAPPDATA%\Colossal Order\Cities_Skylines\Cities_Data\output_log.txt[/code]
