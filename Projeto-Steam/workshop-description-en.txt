[h1]Improved Public Transport 4[/h1]

[b]One mod instead of fifteen.[/b] Real control over every transit line — how many vehicles run, which ones, where they stop, what a ticket costs — with a shelf of small transit mods folded in so they stop fighting.

[b]Version 4.7.0[/b] · Built for game version 1.21.1-f9

[hr][/hr]

[h1]Why this exists[/h1]

A city was bleeding money — transit maintenance had run away to roughly [b]-42,000,000,000[/b]. Neither mod involved was broken. Two working mods were each deciding, independently, how big every line's fleet should be, overwriting each other until the fleet and its upkeep grew without limit.

You cannot fix that by disabling one, since both were doing their job. The fix was to put them in one place, coordinating through one system. That is IPT4.

[hr][/hr]

[h1]What you can do with it[/h1]

[h2]Take charge of any line[/h2]
Bus, tram, metro, train, ship, plane, monorail, cable car, taxi, tour — every line gets a full control panel:

[list]
[*][b]Set the fleet size yourself[/b], or let the mod size it from the budget. A manual choice is never silently overridden.
[*][b]Choose which vehicles run[/b], so city trams stay on city routes and intercity trains stay intercity.
[*][b]Edit vehicles individually[/b], and [b]pick the depot[/b] for each line instead of letting the game guess.
[/list]

[h2]Fix bunching[/h2]
Four buses nose to tail, then nothing for ten minutes. Three levels you can reason about: [b]Disabled[/b] (vanilla), [b]Prudent[/b] (gentle spacing, safe anywhere), [b]Realistic[/b] (holds vehicles at stops to keep true intervals). Set separately for buses and trams — or use [b]Express[/b] mode and have vehicles skip empty stops instead of waiting.

[h2]Set your own fares[/h2]
Price each transport type independently in the [b]Ticket Prices[/b] tab of the Economy panel, which shows the resulting fare. Optional [b]day and night pricing[/b]. Prices feed into demand: cheap transit fills up, expensive transit empties out.

[h2]See what is happening[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Stop info panel[/b] — waiting passengers, next vehicle, how crowded the stop is.
[*][b]Train Display[/b] — follow a vehicle for a live route overlay. Position, size, transparency, fields and update rate all configurable.
[*][b]Automatic line colours and names[/b], so your transport map stays readable.
[*][b]Flight Tracker[/b] for airport arrivals and departures.
[/list]

[h2]Clean up your network[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Bulk line deletion[/b] — every line of a chosen type at once, with a confirmation step.
[*][b]Better stop placement[/b], including on elevated track.
[*][b]Unstucker[/b] — stuck vehicles get released instead of blocking a route.
[/list]

[h2]New in 4.7[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Sub-Buildings Tabs[/b] — a tab bar on any multi-building hub's info panel, not just airports.
[*][b]Commuter Destination[/b] — see where the passengers waiting at a stop are actually headed.
[*][b]Shared Stop Enabler[/b] (off by default) — more than one stop type on the same road segment.
[*][b]Taxi Stand Fix[/b] — idle taxis head for the nearest stand instead of wandering.
[*][b]Intercity bus terminal capacity mode[/b] — Disabled, Intermediate or Realistic, in Options.
[*][b]Train Display “Original” theme[/b], matching the source mod's own look, and corrected colours on the other three themes.
[/list]

[h1]Requirements[/h1]

[b]Harmony[/b] (ID 2040656402) is required. Nothing else — everything the mod needs ships in the download.

[b]Not compatible with:[/b] IPT2 and IPT3 (this replaces them), Transport Lines Manager (same line data), standalone AutoLineBudget, and any standalone copy of the mods above. The mod warns you in-game if it finds one.

[hr][/hr]

[h1]Languages[/h1]

[b]All 23 languages are fully translated[/b] — every string in the mod, not just the menus. It follows your game language, or pick one in the options.

If something reads awkwardly in yours, report it — the files are plain text on GitHub.

[hr][/hr]

[h1]Getting started[/h1]

Subscribe to [b]Harmony[/b], unsubscribe from IPT2/IPT3 and the standalone mods above, then enable IPT4 in Content Manager. Load a city and click any transit line.

Options live under [b]Settings → Mod Settings → Improved Public Transport 4[/b]; fares are in the game's [b]Economy[/b] panel.

[b]Existing saves work.[/b] IPT4 reads the same save data IPT2 and IPT3 wrote, so per-line fleet sizes, depots and vehicle restrictions carry over.

[hr][/hr]

[h1]Stability, and what to do if something breaks[/h1]

No longer marked BETA. The runaway-maintenance bug is fixed at its root, and the mod repairs saves it already damaged: on load it clears only the negative transit income and expense entries left behind and corrects the matching totals. Valid history and other services are untouched.

Even so, [b]keep a backup save[/b] the first time you load a large city with any new transit mod. If something goes wrong, say [i]what you were doing[/i] and attach your log:
[code]%LOCALAPPDATA%\Colossal Order\Cities_Skylines\Cities_Data\output_log.txt[/code]

“Doesn’t work” with no log cannot be diagnosed. With one, it usually can.
