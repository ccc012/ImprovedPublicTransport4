[h1]Improved Public Transport 4[/h1]

[b]One mod instead of fifteen.[/b] Real control over every transit line — fleet size, which vehicles run, where they stop, what a ticket costs — small transit mods folded in so they stop fighting.

[b]Version 4.8.0[/b] · Built for game version 1.21.1-f9

[hr][/hr]

[h1]Why this exists[/h1]

A city was bleeding money — transit maintenance ran away to roughly [b]-42,000,000,000[/b]. Neither mod involved was broken; two working mods were each deciding, independently, how big every line's fleet should be, overwriting each other until upkeep grew without limit. Disabling one is not a fix, since both were doing their job — the fix is one place, one system. That is IPT4.

One assembly patching each hooked method once, instead of several mods each patching it separately, also tends to run lighter: informal single-machine testing measured ~50-60 FPS with IPT4 against ~40-50 FPS running the same feature set as separate mods (or the predecessor IPT3). Not a controlled benchmark, but a fair expectation of the general direction.

[hr][/hr]

[h1]What you can do with it[/h1]

[h2]Take charge of any line[/h2]
Bus, tram, metro, train, ship, plane, monorail, cable car, taxi, tour — every line gets a full control panel:

[list]
[*][b]Set the fleet size yourself[/b], or let the mod size it from the budget — never silently overridden.
[*][b]Choose which vehicles run[/b], so city trams stay on city routes and intercity trains stay intercity.
[*][b]Edit vehicles individually[/b] and [b]pick the depot[/b] per line.
[/list]

[h2]Fix bunching[/h2]
Four buses nose to tail, then nothing for ten minutes. Three levels, set separately for buses and trams: [b]Disabled[/b] (vanilla), [b]Prudent[/b] (gentle spacing), [b]Realistic[/b] (true intervals). Or use [b]Express[/b] mode to skip empty stops.

[h2]Set your own fares[/h2]
Price each transport type independently in the [b]Ticket Prices[/b] tab of the Economy panel, with optional [b]day/night pricing[/b]. Prices feed demand: cheap transit fills up, expensive transit empties out.

[h2]See what is happening[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Stop info panel[/b] — waiting passengers, next vehicle, crowding.
[*][b]Train Display[/b] — live route overlay while following a vehicle, fully configurable.
[*][b]Automatic line colours and names[/b] for a readable transit map.
[*][b]Flight Tracker[/b] for airport arrivals and departures.
[/list]

[h2]Clean up your network[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Bulk line deletion[/b] — every line of a chosen type at once, with confirmation.
[*][b]Better stop placement[/b], including on elevated track.
[*][b]Unstucker[/b] — stuck vehicles get released instead of blocking a route.
[/list]

[h2]New in 4.8[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Single Train Track AI[/b] — shared single-track rail segments are reserved for one direction at a time, so two trains no longer risk colliding.
[*][b]Stop Stacker[/b] — a second bus converging on the same stop gets its own berth further back instead of queuing behind the first.
[*][b]Intercity Bus Control[/b] fixed at the root — the DLC check was unreliable and could silently disable the whole feature even for players who own Sunset Harbor; now on by default.
[*][b]Sub-Buildings Tabs[/b] — fixed a rare stale-tab bug after demolishing/rebuilding; now on by default.
[*]Bug and performance sweep across several integrations — see the technical changelog for the full list.
[/list]

[h2]New in 4.7[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Sub-Buildings Tabs[/b] — tab bar on any multi-building hub, not just airports.
[*][b]Commuter Destination[/b] — see where waiting passengers are headed.
[*][b]Shared Stop Enabler[/b] (off by default) — share a road segment between stop types.
[*][b]Taxi Stand Fix[/b] — idle taxis head for the nearest stand instead of wandering.
[*][b]Intercity terminal capacity mode[/b] — Disabled, Intermediate or Realistic.
[*][b]Train Display “Original” theme[/b], plus corrected colours elsewhere.
[/list]

[h1]Requirements & compatibility[/h1]

[b]Harmony[/b] (ID 2040656402) required, nothing else. [b]Not compatible with:[/b] IPT2, IPT3, Transport Lines Manager, standalone AutoLineBudget, or a standalone copy of any mod above — warns you in-game if it finds one.

[hr][/hr]

[h1]Languages[/h1]

[b]All 23 languages are fully translated[/b] — every string, not just the menus. Follows your game language, or pick one in the options. Something read awkwardly? Files are plain text on GitHub.

[hr][/hr]

[h1]Getting started[/h1]

Subscribe to [b]Harmony[/b], unsubscribe the standalone mods above, enable IPT4 in Content Manager, load a city and click any transit line. Options live under [b]Settings → Mod Settings → Improved Public Transport 4[/b]; fares are in the [b]Economy[/b] panel. [b]Existing saves work[/b] — same save data as IPT2/IPT3, so per-line settings carry over.

[hr][/hr]

[h1]Stability[/h1]

Stable channel. The runaway-maintenance bug that motivated this fork is fixed at its root, and damaged saves are repaired automatically on load. [b]Keep a backup save[/b] regardless, the first time you load a large city with a new transit mod. If something breaks, say [i]what you were doing[/i] and attach your log:
[code]%LOCALAPPDATA%\Colossal Order\Cities_Skylines\Cities_Data\output_log.txt[/code]
“Doesn’t work” with no log cannot be diagnosed.
