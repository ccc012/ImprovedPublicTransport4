[h1]Improved Public Transport 4[/h1]
[b]One mod instead of fifteen.[/b] Real control over every transit line — fleet size, vehicle types, stops, fares — small transit mods folded in so they stop fighting.
[b]Version 4.8.5[/b] · Built for game version 1.21.1-f9

[h1]Why this exists[/h1]
A city was bleeding money — transit maintenance ran away to roughly [b]-42,000,000,000[/b]. Neither mod involved was broken; two working mods were each deciding, independently, how big every line's fleet should be, overwriting each other. The fix is one place, one system. That is IPT4.
[hr][/hr]
[h1]What you can do with it[/h1]
[h2]Take charge of any line[/h2]
Bus, tram, metro, train, ship, plane, monorail, cable car, taxi, tour — every line gets a full control panel: [b]set the fleet size yourself[/b] or let the mod size it from the budget (never silently overridden), [b]choose which vehicles run[/b] on each line, [b]edit vehicles individually[/b] and [b]pick the depot[/b] per line.
[h2]Fix bunching[/h2]
Four buses nose to tail, then nothing for ten minutes. Three levels, per line: [b]Disabled[/b] (vanilla), [b]Prudent[/b] (gentle spacing), [b]Realistic[/b] (true intervals). Or use [b]Express[/b] mode to skip empty stops.
[h2]Set your own fares[/h2]
Price each transport type independently in the [b]Ticket Prices[/b] tab of the Economy panel, with optional [b]day/night pricing[/b]. Prices feed demand: cheap transit fills up, expensive transit empties out.
[h2]See what is happening[/h2]
[b]Stop info panel[/b] (waiting passengers, next vehicle, crowding), [b]Train Display[/b] (live route overlay while following a vehicle, fully configurable), [b]automatic line colours and names[/b] for a readable transit map, and [b]Flight Tracker[/b] for airport arrivals and departures.
[h2]Clean up your network[/h2]
[b]Bulk line deletion[/b] (every line of a chosen type at once, with confirmation), [b]better stop placement[/b] including on elevated track, and an [b]unstucker[/b] that releases stuck vehicles instead of letting them block a route.
[h2]New in 4.8[/h2]
[b]Single Train Track AI[/b] — a shared single-track rail segment is reserved for one direction at a time, so two trains no longer risk colliding. [b]Stop Stacker[/b] — a second bus converging on the same stop gets its own berth further back instead of queuing behind the first. [b]Intercity Bus Control[/b] fixed at the root — the DLC check was unreliable and could silently disable it even for owners of Sunset Harbor. [b]Sub-Buildings Tabs[/b] — fixed a rare stale-tab bug after demolish/rebuild. Fresh installs use the [b]Safe[/b] profile (everything optional off); pick Recommended or Realistic in Options, or enable individual integrations. Plus a bug and performance sweep — see the technical changelog for the full list.

[h1]Requirements & compatibility[/h1]
[b]Harmony[/b] (ID 2040656402) required. [b]Not compatible with:[/b] IPT2, IPT3, Transport Lines Manager, standalone AutoLineBudget, or a standalone copy of any mod above — warns you in-game if found.
[hr][/hr]
[h1]Languages[/h1]
[b]All 23 languages fully translated[/b] — every string, not just the menus. Follows your game language, or pick one in the options.
[hr][/hr]
[h1]Getting started[/h1]
Subscribe to [b]Harmony[/b], unsubscribe the standalone mods above, enable IPT4, load a city and click any transit line. Options live under [b]Settings → Mod Settings → Improved Public Transport 4[/b]; fares are in the [b]Economy[/b] panel. [b]Existing saves work[/b] — same save data as IPT2/IPT3.
[hr][/hr]
[h1]Stability[/h1]
Stable channel. The runaway-maintenance bug that motivated this fork is fixed at its root, and damaged saves are repaired automatically on load. [b]Keep a backup save[/b] regardless. If something breaks, say [i]what you were doing[/i] and attach:
[code]%LOCALAPPDATA%\Colossal Order\Cities_Skylines\Cities_Data\output_log.txt[/code]
“Doesn’t work” with no log cannot be diagnosed.
