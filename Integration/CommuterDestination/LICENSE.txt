This folder contains a new IPT4 integration layer adapted from the Steam Workshop
item 2475986859, Commuter Destination, by jkm
(github.com/Jameskmonger/CSL-ShowCommuterDestination).

Source origin, credits, and licensing notes:
- The upstream mod is MIT licensed.
- The core destination-graph logic (Graph/*.cs, CitizenDestination.cs,
  GetCitizenDestinations in this folder) is ported closely, including its
  citizen-grid scan and the "TransportArriveAtTarget"-derived stop matching -
  it is read-only (never writes simulation state) and was reviewed line by line.
- The IPT2 integration (Harmony/Patches/IPT2/*) was NOT ported. Upstream hides
  its own panel and IL-transpiles a label into IPT2's PublicTransportStopWorldInfoPanel
  when IPT2 is detected. IPT4 does not need that trick: this integration is built
  INTO IPT4, so it patches IPT4's own UI/PublicTransportStopWorldInfoPanel.cs
  directly with a plain Harmony postfix (adds a button, no transpiler, no
  IL pattern matching) instead of hiding behind another mod's panel.
- The original mod auto-opens its panel on every single stop click
  (OpenStopDestinationPanelPatch on PublicTransportStopButton.OnMouseDown). IPT4
  already opens its own stop panel on that same click - stacking a second
  automatic popup on the same click would show two panels at once. This
  integration instead adds an on-demand button inside IPT4's existing stop
  panel, and only opens when clicked.
