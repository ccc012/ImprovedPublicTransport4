Ported into Improved Public Transport 4 from
https://github.com/algernon-A/UnlimitedOutsideConnections-Revisited (Workshop
item 2367735356, "Unlimited Outside Connections Revisited"), adapted to
IPT4's own PatchController Activate/Deactivate wiring and logging. The
transpiler that clears the "too many connections" tool error was verified
against the actual current game IL for RoadAI/TrainTrackAI/ShipPathAI/
FlightPathAI.GetInfo and is unchanged from upstream: it already used
Harmony's semantic CodeInstruction.LoadsConstant(...) match rather than a
raw opcode scan, which is the same standard this fork holds its own
transpilers to.
